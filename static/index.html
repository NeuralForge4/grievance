Public grievance redressal

Every citizen email is read. The urgent ones are answered first.

Officials receive far more email than anyone can read. A flood alert, a court notice and a thank-you note look the same in an inbox. Grievance AI reads each message, scores its urgency, and puts the most serious cases at the top of your dashboard.

The problem

Important emails get lost in volume.

📬
Too many emails

Officials cannot read every message, so serious ones get buried.

⏳
Delayed response

Emergencies, pension delays and legal notices wait behind routine mail.

🙁
Citizens left waiting

People write again and again, and lose trust when nobody replies.

How it works

Three steps, with a reason shown for every decision.

  1. Collect: emails arrive from the department mailbox automatically.
  2. Understand: an AI model and a trained local model read each email in English or Hindi, and a safety rule keeps life-risk cases from ranking too low.
  3. Prioritise and route: each email gets a level, a reason, a department and a response time. Uncertain ones go to the review queue.
  4. Act: the officer resolves, escalates or corrects the priority, and every action is logged.

Each level has a target response time.

What officials get

Built so a person always stays in charge.

🙋 Human review queue

Unsure or conflicting cases are flagged for an officer instead of guessed.

🛡️ Privacy first

Frequently asked questions

Who can log in?

Only registered government officials. Citizens continue to write to the usual official email address.

Does the AI make the final decision?

No. It ranks emails and explains why. The officer can change any priority, escalate or resolve.

Can it read Hindi emails?

Yes. The prototype recognises common Hindi urgency and category words.

Is this connected to a real inbox?

Yes. A collector reads the department mailbox over IMAP and stores each email in the database.
